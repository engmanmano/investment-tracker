Portfolio Holdings & Rationale — May 2026
Overall Strategy
The portfolio is built for long‑term, consistent wealth accumulation through a diversified mix of global and Philippine assets. Allocations are automated across two monthly paydays, removing emotion and market timing. Low costs, tax advantages, and broad diversification drive the approach.

Holdings by Role
MP2 (Pag-IBIG MP2)
Role: Sovereign-backed anchor.
Why: Tax‑free, ~7% annual dividend, zero correlation with equity markets. Contributions are split between a maturing account (2023–2028) and a rollover. Provides a stable, government‑guaranteed compounding layer.

VWRA (Vanguard FTSE All‑World UCITS ETF)
Role: Core global passive index.
Why: Full developed and emerging market equity exposure in a single, low‑cost (0.22% ER) Irish‑domiciled ETF. Cash is staged in a high‑yield savings account and deployed quarterly to minimize trading fees. This is the portfolio’s primary growth engine and global diversification tool.

Role: PH mining growth.
Why: Operator of the Didipio gold‑copper mine, benefiting from rising commodity prices. Accumulated in board lots inside the PERA tax shield. Adds direct commodity exposure and an inflation hedge.

Role: PH energy & infrastructure.
Why: Holds the concession for the national transmission grid — a toll‑bridge business with stable, regulated returns. Accumulated in board lots inside PERA. Provides essential‑service, non‑discretionary demand exposure.

Role: PH banking compounder.
Why: A well‑managed major Philippine bank, positioned to benefit from domestic economic growth and rising rates. PERA position receives monthly contributions; a separate 10‑share COL position keeps a legacy brokerage account active.
